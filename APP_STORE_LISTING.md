## Description
```
EEIS Prayer Times is the official prayer companion for the Epsom & Ewell Islamic Society.

Accurate daily prayer times with begins (adhan) and Jama'at (iqamah) times for Fajr, Shuruq,
Dhuhr/Jummah, Asr, Maghrib and Isha.

FEATURES
• Daily prayer times with a live countdown to the next prayer
• Adhan alarms you can set per prayer, with a choice of sounds
• Friday Jummah times
• Qibla compass that points toward Mecca (works on your device — no internet needed)
• Gregorian and Hijri (Islamic) date
• Daily Quran & Hadith reminders
• World Times — prayer times, weather and currency for cities around the world
• Donation and Gift Aid information for supporting the mosque
• Help guide in English, Urdu, Bengali and Arabic

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no sign-in, no advertising, and no tracking. Your settings stay on your device.
Location is used only on your device for the Qibla compass and is never sent anywhere.

EEIS Prayer Times is free to use.
```
