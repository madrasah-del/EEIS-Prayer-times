Privacy Policy — EEIS Prayer Times

Last updated: 22 June 2026

The EEIS Prayer Times app is made for the Epsom & Ewell Islamic Society (“EEIS”) and published by JJ Productions (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your preferences — such as which prayer alerts you enable, chosen sounds, font size, and pinned cities — are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and they are removed if you uninstall the app.

Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, identifiers, or usage analytics. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Location

The app can use your device location for one purpose only: to point the Qibla compass toward Mecca. This calculation happens entirely on your device. Your location is never transmitted to us or any third party, and is not stored. Granting location permission is optional; the rest of the app works without it.

Content the app downloads

To show prayer times, announcements, and Quran/Hadith reminders, the app downloads public content files from our online repository. To show the optional “World Times” information, the app requests weather and exchange-rate data from third-party services (Open-Meteo, AlAdhan, FloatRates) using fixed city coordinates only — not your location. As with any internet request, the receiving server sees standard technical information such as your IP address; we do not receive or store this.

Notifications and alarms

Prayer-time alerts and the adhan alarm are scheduled and played locally on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, if a future version adds a members’ area that handles personal data), we will update this page and the “last updated” date, and describe what data is involved before that feature is released.
